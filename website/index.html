OpenPGP for your Mac.
Keys, files, and Mail.

RNP brings OpenPGP encryption to macOS. Manage your keyring, encrypt and decrypt files, and add lock icons to encrypted and signed email in Apple Mail. Powered by librnp, Thunderbird's official end-to-end encryption engine.

⬇ Download for macOS
macOS 14 or later · Free · Open source · brew install --cask rnp
🔑

Key Management

Generate, import, rotate, and revoke OpenPGP keys. Touch ID for keyring unlock. Hybrid post-quantum algorithms supported.

📄

File Encrypt & Sign

Drag a file in, encrypt to recipients, sign with your key, or verify someone else's signature. Finder Quick Action included.

✉️

RNP for Mail

Apple Mail extension shows lock icons on encrypted and signed messages. Autocrypt-aware. WKD key discovery built in.

🔍

Key Discovery

Auto-fetch recipient keys via WKD (Web Key Directory) and keys.openpgp.org. Autocrypt headers honored inbound.

🛡

Sandboxed

Properly sandboxed with App Group keyring. Hardened Runtime. Ready for Mac App Store. No telemetry, ever.

🌐

Standards-based

OpenPGP, PGP/MIME (RFC 3156), Autocrypt, WKD, and the crypto-refresh. PQ hybrids (ML-DSA, ML-KEM) supported.

Powered by
librnp
The same engine that powers Thunderbird's official end-to-end encryption. Battle-tested, audited, and actively maintained by the Ribose team.
